DocFinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What DocFinder accesses

With the account owner's permission, DocFinder uses read-only Google scopes (Gmail read-only and Google Drive read-only) to search and display the owner's own emails, attachments and Drive files.

Where data goes

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Search indexes and access tokens are stored locally. Nothing is sent to the developer, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DocFinder's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting DocFinder's local folder removes all stored data.
